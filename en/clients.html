Clients

Clients I built a brand from scratch for, and others who needed their face given back to them. Logotypes, colour and type systems, naming when it is needed, and everything that holds an identity up afterwards.

There are architecture studios, corner pharmacies, family wineries and a startup that changed its name twice before finding its own. The criterion never changed: the piece has to survive daily use, a bad photocopy and an old phone screen.
